How to mirror or flip an image

  1. Select your picture.
  2. Tap Flip horizontal to make a mirror image, left to right.
  3. Tap Flip vertical to flip it top to bottom.
  4. Download the result.

Flip versus rotate

Rotating turns the picture around, so left stays left. Flipping creates a mirror image, so left and right swap. Text in a flipped picture reads backward.

When a mirror image helps

Flipping is useful for selfies that were saved mirrored, for matching a subject to the side of a layout, and for making a reflection effect. You can combine a flip with rotation, and Reset brings the original back.

Good uses for a flipped picture

  • Fixing a selfie that was saved as a mirror image.
  • Making a subject face the other way in a layout.
  • Creating a simple reflection or symmetry effect.

Frequently asked questions

How do I mirror an image online?

Select the picture and tap Flip horizontal. The left and right sides swap, giving you a mirror image to download.

Does flipping change image quality?

The picture is saved again at high quality, so any change is very small for photos.

Can I flip and rotate the same picture?

Yes. Use the flip and rotate buttons in any order, then download. Reset returns the image to its original position.
