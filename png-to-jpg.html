How to convert PNG to JPG

  1. Select your PNG file.
  2. Check that Convert to is set to JPG.
  3. Choose a quality, such as 90, and click Convert Image.
  4. Download the JPG.

Why convert PNG to JPG

Many online forms, exam portals, and job sites accept only JPG, and a JPG of a photo is usually many times smaller than the PNG. Converting is often the fastest way to get under a KB limit.

Transparent backgrounds

JPG cannot store transparency. Any transparent area in your PNG becomes white after conversion. If you need to keep a transparent background, stay with PNG or WebP.

Next step for a KB limit

After converting, check the size. If the form allows only 100 KB or 50 KB, use the 100 KB resizer or the 50 KB resizer.

Check the result before you upload

Open the JPG and zoom in on text and sharp edges. If they look soft, convert again at a higher quality, such as 95 or 100 percent. You can also choose a smaller KB limit afterwards with the resizer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a PNG to JPG for an exam form?

Convert it here, then use a KB tool such as the 50 KB or 100 KB page to meet the exact limit. Check the form for the required pixel size too.

How much smaller will the JPG be?

For photos, often a lot smaller, because JPG compresses them far better than PNG. Graphics with flat colors show a smaller gain.

What quality should I choose?

Use 90 to 92 for photos. Lower the number if you need a smaller file, but check the preview for blocky areas.
