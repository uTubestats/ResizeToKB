Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use ResizeToKB. In short: your images are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to us.

Summary

  • We do not upload, see or store the images you resize.
  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We currently use no analytics and show no ads.
  • We only receive your name, email address and message if you choose to email us.

Your Images

The resizer runs in your web browser on your own device. When you select a photo, it is read and changed locally. It is not sent to our servers or to any other service. When you close the page, the working copy is gone. The file you download is saved only on your device.

Information You Send Us

The contact form does not send anything by itself. It opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. If you press send there, we receive the details you included, such as your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply and to improve the site. Please do not email passwords, bank details or identity documents.

How Long We Keep Emails

We keep emails for as long as we need to answer you and to keep a record of requests. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time by writing to the address on the Contact page.

Cookies and Browser Storage

Our own code does not set cookies and does not store personal data in your browser. Your browser may keep normal cached copies of pages and files so the site loads faster.

Hosting and Server Logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address and the pages you request. We do not control these logs. They are covered by GitHub's own privacy statement.

Analytics and Advertising

At the time of writing, ResizeToKB uses no analytics tools and shows no advertisements. If we add advertising, such as Google AdSense, third-party vendors may use cookies to show ads based on earlier visits. If that happens, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Links to Other Websites

Some pages link to other sites, such as MDN Web Docs. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

ResizeToKB is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has emailed us personal details, please contact us and we will delete them.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Because we only hold the emails you send us, you can make any such request by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
