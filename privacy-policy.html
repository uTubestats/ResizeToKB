Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your pictures and to the basic data of this website. The short version: your images are processed in your own browser and are not uploaded to us.

Your images

When you select a photo on ResizeToKB, the resizing, converting, rotating, or compressing runs on your device. We do not upload, store, view, or share your images. The files you download are created on your device as well. This is true for sensitive pictures such as passport photos, signatures, and ID copies.

Information we collect

We do not ask for an account, a name, or a phone number. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard technical data, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you visit. If we add analytics, they will use this kind of data to show us which pages are useful, not to identify you.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads served by Google AdSense or similar partners. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this site and others. You can manage personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although parts of some websites may then work differently.

Third-party links

Some pages link to official exam notices and other websites. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read the policy of any site you visit.

Data retention and your choices

Because we do not store your pictures or personal details, there is nothing to delete on our side. Technical logs kept by our hosting provider follow that provider's policy. If you have a question about your data, write to us at piyt35567@gmail.com.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows, for example when we add analytics or advertising. The date at the top shows the latest update. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us or write to piyt35567@gmail.com.

Security

The site is delivered over HTTPS, and your pictures never travel to our servers, which removes the main privacy risk of image tools. No method is perfect, so avoid processing files on shared or public computers.

Visitors from other countries

The site can be used from anywhere. If your country gives you rights over your data, such as access or deletion, write to us and we will respond as the law requires. Because we do not keep your pictures or personal details, there is usually nothing to delete.
