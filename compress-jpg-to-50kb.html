How to Compress JPG to 50KB

Published 25 October 2026 · by ResizeToKB

Need to compress JPG to 50KB for a job application, visa form or admission portal? There are three easy ways to do it, and this guide shows each one so you can pick what suits your device.

Ways to Compress JPG to 50KB

1. A free online tool (fastest)

  1. Open the Resize Image to 50KB tool.
  2. Select your JPG and confirm the target is 50.
  3. Press Resize Image, check the preview and download.

The tool runs in your browser, so your photo is not uploaded.

2. Windows Paint

Paint can shrink the pixel size, which often reduces the file size. Open the photo, choose Resize, pick Pixels, keep the aspect ratio, and save as JPEG. Then check the file size. Paint has no quality slider, so you may need to try a smaller size more than once.

3. Mac Preview

Open the photo in Preview and choose File, then Export. Pick JPEG and move the Quality slider lower until the size shown is under 50KB.

Which Method Should You Choose?

  • Phone user: use the online tool.
  • Need an exact limit fast: use the online tool.
  • No internet: use Paint or Preview.

Tips After You Compress JPG to 50KB

  • Check the file size in your file manager before you upload.
  • Keep the original in case the form rejects the first try.
  • If the picture looks too soft, crop the empty space and try again.

Common Questions

Is JPG the same as JPEG?

Yes. They are the same format with different names.

Why is my compressed JPG still above 50KB?

The photo may be very detailed or very large. Crop it, reduce the pixel size, then compress again from the original.
