Instagram Post Size 2026: Feed, Story and Grid Dimensions

Updated October 2026

Instagram's profile grid now shows posts as taller 3:4 tiles instead of squares. That changed which size works best. Here are the dimensions most 2026 guides recommend.

Which feed size should you pick?

1080 × 1350 is the safe default. It takes up a lot of screen space in the feed and holds up well in the grid. Choose 1080 × 1440 if you want each tile to match the image with no trimming. Square posts still work, but they show side bars in the 3:4 grid.

Safe zone tip

Keep faces, text, and logos near the center of the image. The grid can trim a little from the top and bottom or the sides, depending on the size you upload.

Frequently asked questions

Does Instagram compress my photo?

Yes. Uploading at 1080 pixels wide usually avoids extra resizing and keeps quality good.

Will my old square posts look wrong?

They still display fine in the feed. In the grid they appear with side bars.

Do sizes change often?

They can. Check Instagram's help pages if something looks off.

Carousels, Reels covers, and profile photos

Carousel posts follow the same sizes as single posts, and all slides in one carousel should use the same ratio so nothing is cropped unexpectedly. Reels play full screen at 1080 × 1920, but the cover can also appear as a 3:4 tile on your grid, so keep titles and faces in the middle. Profile pictures are shown as a circle, so use a square image with the subject centered.

Image quality tips

  • Export at 1080 pixels wide so Instagram does not need to shrink the image.
  • Use JPG for photos and PNG for graphics with sharp text.
  • Avoid placing text close to the edges, where it may be cut off.
  • Look at your post in the feed and in the grid before publishing.

Which size is best for you?

If you post mostly photos from your phone, 1080 × 1350 gives a tall, immersive feed look. If you care about a neat profile grid, 1080 × 1440 matches the tile. If you cross-post to other apps, 1080 × 1080 is a safe shared size. You can keep a few versions of the same image and choose per platform.
