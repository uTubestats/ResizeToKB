FAQ

How do I reduce image size in KB?

Select your photo, open the Target KB tab, type the limit shown on your form, and click Resize Image. The result is a JPG just under that size.

How do I compress an image to a specific size?

Type the exact number in the KB box. The tool lowers JPG quality in small steps, and it shrinks the pixel dimensions only if the quality would otherwise drop too far.

How do I reduce JPG file size online without losing too much quality?

Choose a limit that is as high as the form allows. A higher KB limit keeps more detail, so avoid going lower than you need.

How to change image size in KB when your limit is not a preset

Presets cover the common limits, but forms use all kinds of numbers. On this page, open the Target KB tab, type your own limit, and the tool brings the file just under it. It works for JPG, PNG, and WebP input, and it saves a JPG, PNG, or WebP file depending on your choice.
