Resize image to 10 KB: very small limits explained

A 10 KB limit leaves room for a small thumbnail or a plain signature. Photos with faces can reach it, but they lose detail, so keep the picture simple: even light, a plain background, and a tight crop make a large difference.

  • Use JPG, which compresses photos far better than PNG.
  • Crop away empty space before you resize.
  • If the result looks too soft, check whether the form also allows 15 or 20 KB.

For scanned signatures, sign with a dark pen on white paper. Clean white space compresses to almost nothing, which leaves more of the 10 KB for the signature itself.
