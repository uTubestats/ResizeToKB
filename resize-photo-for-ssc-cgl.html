SSC CGL Photo and Signature Size: What Guides Report

Many exam guides report these values for SSC recruitment forms: a photo of 20–50KB in JPG format, and a signature of about 140×60 pixels (roughly 4×2cm) at 10–20KB. Photo pixel sizes are reported differently from site to site, and some guides say certain SSC registrations capture the photo live by webcam.

Because guides disagree and rules change every year, treat these numbers as a starting point only. Confirm every limit in the official notification, then enter it in Target KB or Pixels mode above.
