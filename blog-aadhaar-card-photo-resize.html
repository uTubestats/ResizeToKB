Aadhaar Card Photo Resize: KB Limits and Upload Tips

Updated October 2026

People search for "Aadhaar card photo resize" for two different reasons, and they need different answers. Some want to change the photo on their Aadhaar. Most simply need to upload a photo or a scanned copy of their Aadhaar to another form and have been told the file is too big. This guide covers both.

Changing the photo on your Aadhaar

The photo on an Aadhaar is captured live at an Aadhaar enrolment or update centre. It is not something you resize and upload online. If you want a new photo on the card, you need to visit a centre, and the official UIDAI website explains the steps and the fee.

Uploading an Aadhaar copy or photo to another form

Banks, job portals, schools, and government services often ask for an Aadhaar copy or a photo with a size limit such as 100 KB, 200 KB, or 2 MB. There is no single limit, because each portal sets its own, so the number on that form is the one to follow.

How to resize a scanned Aadhaar copy

  1. Scan or photograph the card on a flat, plain surface, in even light, with all four corners visible.
  2. Crop away the extra space around the card.
  3. Open the reduce image size in KB tool, type the portal's limit, and click Resize Image.
  4. Zoom into the result to make sure the text and the photo are still readable.

For common limits you can also use the 100 KB and 200 KB pages.

Keep your Aadhaar private

  • Use a tool that resizes in your browser, as ResizeToKB does, so the card is not uploaded to a server.
  • If the form allows it, use the masked Aadhaar from the official UIDAI site, which hides the first digits of the number.
  • Do not share the file in public chats or groups.

If the upload still fails

Check the file type (usually JPG or PDF), the exact size limit, and the image dimensions. If the portal wants a PDF, resize the picture first and then save it as a PDF in your phone's gallery or files app.

Common situations where people need this

  • Bank or KYC upload: the form asks for an Aadhaar copy under a KB limit.
  • Job or exam portal: an ID proof is required next to your photo and signature.
  • School or scholarship form: a scanned copy of the card is needed.
  • Government service portal: a document upload with a size limit such as 200 KB or 1 MB.

Scanning tips for a readable copy

Place the card on a flat, dark or plain surface and photograph it straight from above in even light. Avoid glare from the plastic or paper. Include all four corners and make sure the text and the photo on the card are in focus. A readable copy matters more than a very small file, so start by finding the limit and then reduce the size only as much as needed.

JPG or PDF?

Some portals accept only JPG, others only PDF. If the form wants a PDF, resize the image first, then save or print it as a PDF from your phone's gallery or files app. If the PDF is still too big, go back and use a lower KB limit on the image.

Privacy checklist

  • Resize on your own device, as ResizeToKB does, and avoid sites that upload the file.
  • Delete extra copies from your downloads folder when you are done.
  • Do not post your Aadhaar image on social media or public chats.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Aadhaar card photo size in KB?

There is no single number. Each form sets its own limit, so use the figure shown on that portal.

Can I change my Aadhaar photo online?

No. The photo is captured at an enrolment or update centre. Check the official UIDAI website for the process.

Does ResizeToKB upload my Aadhaar copy?

No. The resizing runs inside your browser and the file stays on your device.
