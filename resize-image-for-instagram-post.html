When You Need to Resize Image for Instagram Post

Instagram shows feed posts as squares or portrait pictures, and it crops anything that does not fit. A 1080×1080 image fills the square cleanly. Instagram also accepts a taller 4:5 shape (1080×1350), which takes more room on the screen.

Resize Image for Instagram Post: Common Mistakes

  • Posting a wide landscape photo and letting Instagram crop the sides.
  • Stretching a photo into a square. Crop it first, or keep the ratio.
  • Uploading a tiny image. It will look soft when the app enlarges it.

Resize Image for Instagram Post: Common Questions

What size is an Instagram post?

1080×1080 pixels is the common square size. Instagram also supports other ratios, but this is a reliable default.

Can I post a photo that is not square?

Yes. Instagram supports other shapes, such as a portrait 4:5 picture. Square 1080×1080 is the safest choice when you want no surprises.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Resizing runs in your browser, so your image is not sent to a server.
