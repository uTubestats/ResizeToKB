Frequently asked questions

How do I resize an image to 100KB?

Upload your image, open Target KB, enter 100, and click Resize. The tool reduces quality and size until the file is just under 100KB.

Is this image resizer free and private?

Yes. It is free with no signup, and images are processed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Will resizing reduce image quality?

Making an image smaller keeps it sharp. Reducing the file size in KB uses compression, so keep the target reasonable to avoid visible loss.

Which formats are supported?

You can open JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF files, and save the result as JPG, PNG or WebP.
