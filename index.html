Frequently asked questions

How do I resize an image to 100KB?

Select your image, open Target KB, enter 100, and press Resize Image. The tool reduces quality and size until the file is just under 100KB.

Does resizing reduce image quality?

Changing pixel size keeps an image sharp when you shrink it. Reducing the file size in KB uses compression, so very small targets can look softer.

Is this image resizer free and private?

Yes. It is free with no signup, and processing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Which formats are supported?

You can open JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF files, and save the result as JPG, WebP or PNG.

Why is my PNG not getting smaller in Target KB mode?

PNG files do not shrink by lowering quality. In Target KB mode the tool saves a JPG so it can reach your size.
