Resize by pixels or percentage

Pixels and percentage change how big the picture is on screen. Choose resize image by pixels when a form or design gives you exact numbers, or resize image by percentage when you just want a smaller or larger copy. The tool opens JPG, PNG, and WebP files and saves a JPG, PNG, or WebP.

For the sharpest result, shrink rather than enlarge, and always work from your original photo. If you are not sure which tool to use, read how to compress an image to a specific KB size or how to resize an image to exact pixel dimensions.

What is the difference between resizing to pixels and resizing to KB?

Pixels set the width and height of the picture. KB sets the file size. A form may ask for either one or both, and this tool handles each.
