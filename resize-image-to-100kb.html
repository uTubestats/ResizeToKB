FAQ

How do I resize image to 100KB?

Upload your image and click Resize. The tool is already set to 100KB, so you only need to download the result.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Your picture is processed on your device and never sent to a server, so it stays private.

Which format should I choose?

JPG is the best choice for photos and small files. PNG keeps every pixel, so it cannot be squeezed to an exact KB limit.

Can I compress a PNG to 100 KB?

PNG is lossless, so quality cannot be lowered to hit a size. Choose JPG or WebP in the Save as menu, and the tool can bring the file under 100 KB much more easily.

How do I compress an image to 100 KB without losing quality?

Start from the original photo and crop out extra space. The tool lowers quality only as far as needed, and it shrinks the pixel dimensions only when quality alone is not enough.

Photo size 100 KB for forms, portals and email

100 KB is a comfortable middle ground. It is small enough for most portals, yet large enough to keep a face sharp and the text on a scanned document readable. Many job sites and student portals use it as their upload cap.

Change image size to 100 KB online without losing clarity

This tool lowers JPG quality in small steps and only reduces the pixel dimensions when it has to. That order protects sharpness better than shrinking the picture first. For a scanned document, zoom into the preview and check that small text is still readable before you upload.

A very large original, such as a multi-megabyte photo from a recent phone, works fine. You will get better results from the original than from a copy that was already compressed once.
