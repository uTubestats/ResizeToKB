How to Resize Image to 100KB

Published 25 October 2026 · by ResizeToKB

If you need to resize image to 100KB for a form, the quickest fix is to compress the photo in your browser. This guide shows the steps and explains why some photos need more than one change.

Why You May Need to Resize Image to 100KB

  • Job, school and government portals often set a maximum file size.
  • Email services can reject large attachments.
  • Smaller files upload faster on slow mobile data.

Steps to Resize Image to 100KB

  1. Open the Resize Image to 100KB tool.
  2. Tap Select Image and choose your photo.
  3. Check that Target KB is selected and the number is 100.
  4. Press Resize Image and look at the preview.
  5. Tap Download Image, then check the file size in your gallery or file manager.

What to Do If the Photo Looks Blurry

  • Save as JPG or WebP. They usually give smaller files than PNG for photos.
  • Crop away empty space before you resize.
  • Check the wording of the limit. If it says "up to 100KB", a result of 90KB is fine.
  • Start again from your original photo, not from an already compressed copy.

Common Mistakes

  • Renaming a file from .png to .jpg does not change its size or format.
  • Saving the same JPG many times lowers quality each time.
  • Ignoring a pixel rule. Some forms need exact width and height as well as a size limit.

How Target KB Mode Works

The tool saves your picture at a high quality, then checks the file size. If it is above 100KB, it tries lower quality settings and keeps the best one that fits. If even the lowest quality is too large, it shrinks the pixel size a little and tries again. This is why the result is usually just under your limit.

Resize Image to 100KB on Android and iPhone

  • Open the tool in your phone browser, such as Chrome or Safari.
  • Tap Select Image and pick a photo from your gallery.
  • After resizing, tap Download Image. The file goes to your Downloads folder or Files app.
  • To check the size on Android, open the file details in Files. On iPhone, open the Files app and use Get Info.

Quick Checklist

  • Original photo saved safely.
  • JPG or WebP chosen.
  • Preview checked.
  • Final size and pixel rules checked on the form.

Common Questions

Is 100KB the same as 0.1MB?

About. One megabyte is roughly 1000 kilobytes, so 100KB is about 0.1MB.

Why is my result smaller than 100KB?

The tool picks the highest quality that still fits. A simple photo can be well under the limit even at high quality.
