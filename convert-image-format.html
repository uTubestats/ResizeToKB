How to convert an image between JPG, PNG, and WebP

  1. Select the picture you want to convert.
  2. Pick the new format in the Convert to menu.
  3. Leave the quality at 92 percent for photos, or lower it for a smaller file.
  4. Click Convert Image and download the result.

Good to know

Converting does not add detail. A low quality JPG saved as PNG is still low quality, only bigger. Converting a transparent PNG to JPG fills the transparent areas with white. An animated GIF uses only its first frame. For a file size limit in KB, convert first and then use the reduce image size in KB tool.

Tips for better conversions

  • Start from the original file, not from a copy that was already converted several times.
  • Use 90 to 92 percent quality for photos, and 100 percent when the picture has fine text.
  • Convert once. Repeated conversions between lossy formats add small errors each time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a PNG to JPG without losing quality?

Keep the quality at 90 percent or higher. JPG is lossy, but at that level the difference is hard to see on photos. Logos and text with sharp edges can show small artifacts.

Can I convert WebP to JPG on my phone?

Yes. Open this page in your phone browser, select the WebP file from your gallery, choose JPG, and download the result.

Does converting an image change its file size?

Yes. JPG and WebP are usually much smaller than PNG for photos, while converting JPG to PNG usually makes the file larger.
