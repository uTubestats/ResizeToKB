SSC CGL Photo and Signature Size 2026: Pixels, KB and How to Resize

Updated October 2026

Wrong photo or signature size is one of the most common reasons an exam form upload fails. Here are the sizes listed in 2026 guides for SSC CGL, and a quick way to fix your files.

SSC CGL photo and signature size

PhotoSignature
Dimensions275 × 354 px236 × 79 px
File size20–50 KB10–20 KB
FormatJPGJPG

Important: exam rules change from year to year. Always confirm the numbers in the official SSC notification before you upload. Some guides also say the photo must be captured live, so read the instructions on the application page.

How to resize your SSC photo

  1. Open the SSC CGL photo resizer.
  2. Select your photo. A clear, recent photo with a plain light background works best.
  3. Click Resize Image. The tool sets 275 × 354 pixels and keeps the file under 50KB.
  4. Check the result size shown, then download the JPG.

How to resize your signature

  1. Sign on plain white paper with a dark pen.
  2. Take a clear, well-lit photo or scan it, then crop the extra paper.
  3. Open the SSC CGL signature resizer, select the file, and click Resize Image.
  4. Download the result. It is set to 236 × 79 pixels and under 20KB.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using an old photo that does not match the notice.
  • Uploading the wrong dimensions or a file over the size limit.
  • A blurry or dark signature scan.
  • Saving as PNG when the form asks for JPG.

Why a photo ends up too small

If the result is below the minimum (for example under 20KB), your photo may be very simple or the lighting too flat. Use a sharper original and try again.

Checklist before you upload

  1. Read the current notification and note the exact pixels, KB range, and format.
  2. Prepare a recent photo with a plain light background, your face centered, and no glasses glare or shadows.
  3. Resize the photo, then check that the file size shown is inside the allowed range.
  4. Resize the signature the same way and make sure the ink is dark and fully visible.
  5. Upload both files and confirm the preview on the form looks clear.

Why uploads get rejected

Most rejections come down to four things: the file is larger than the maximum, the image is smaller than the minimum, the format is not JPG, or the picture does not match the rules (for example an old photo). A fifth cause is a blurry signature, which some portals flag even when the size is correct. Fixing these before you upload saves you from repeating the form.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a photo taken on my phone?

Yes, if the notification allows a normal photo. Take it in daylight against a plain wall, then resize it to the listed pixels and KB range. If the form requires a live capture, follow that instead.

What if my file is below the minimum size?

A very simple or low-detail image can end up smaller than the minimum. Use a sharper, higher-quality original and resize again so the result lands inside the range.

Should I save as PNG or JPG?

Use JPG. The sizes in the guides are listed for JPG, and JPG can be adjusted to hit a KB limit.

Do the rules change each year?

They can. Check the official notice every time you apply, even if you filled a form last year.
