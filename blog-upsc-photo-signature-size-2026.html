UPSC Photo and Signature Size 2026 in KB and Pixels

Updated October 2026

The UPSC application is strict about photos and signatures, and recent notices added a live photograph step. This guide lists what 2026 guides report and shows how to prepare the files, but you should always confirm the numbers in the official instructions that came with your notification.

UPSC photo and signature rules listed for 2026

PhotographSignature

Guides also report that capturing a live photograph during the application is mandatory in 2026, and that typed or digital signatures are not accepted. One guide lists a wider photo range of 20 to 300 KB, so the official instructions decide.

How to prepare the photo

  1. Use a recent, clear colour photo in front of a plain white background.
  2. Open the reduce image size in KB tool, type 200, and click Resize Image.
  3. Check that the file is at least 20 KB, and rename it photo.jpg.

How to prepare the triple signature

  1. Sign three times on one white sheet with black ink, leaving space between the signatures.
  2. Photograph the sheet straight from above and crop close to the signatures.
  3. Keep both sides between 350 and 500 pixels using the pixels tool.
  4. Use the 100 KB tool so the file is under 100 KB, then rename it signature.jpg.

Common reasons for rejection

  • A photo that does not match the live photograph.
  • A signature file outside 20 to 100 KB or outside the pixel range.
  • A PNG renamed to JPG, which the portal can still detect.
  • Missing or wrong file names.

Preparing the three-signature image

Take a white sheet of paper and sign three times in a row with black ink, leaving a clear gap between the signatures. Each signature should look like your normal one. Photograph the sheet straight from above in even light, then crop close to the three signatures so no big empty area is left.

Photo quality checklist

  • A recent colour photo, with your face covering most of the frame, as the guides describe.
  • A plain white or light background and even light on your face.
  • No glare on glasses, no cap, and no filters.
  • The file is a JPG named exactly as the instructions say.

If the portal asks for specific file names

Some UPSC instructions list exact file names for the uploads. Rename your files before you upload them, and keep the .jpg ending. A correct picture with a wrong name can still be refused.

Check the numbers every year

UPSC updates its instructions from time to time, and several guides disagree on details such as the upper photo limit. Keep a copy of the instructions you followed, so you can compare them with the next notification.

Guides list 20 to 100 KB, JPG, with all three signatures in one image and dimensions of 350 to 500 pixels.

Recent guides report that capturing a live photo is mandatory for 2026. Check the notification for your exam.
