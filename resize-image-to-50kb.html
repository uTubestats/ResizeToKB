FAQ

How do I resize image to 50KB?

Upload your image and click Resize. The tool is already set to 50KB, so you only need to download the result.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. All the work happens in your browser tab, and the photo is not stored anywhere.

Which format should I choose?

Pick JPG when you need a small photo file. Use PNG only for logos or graphics, since it cannot be tuned to a KB target.

How do I compress a photo to 50 KB without losing quality?

Some detail is always lost, but you can limit it: crop first, start from the original, and save as JPG. The tool then picks the highest quality that still fits under 50 KB.

Can I compress a JPG to 50 KB online for free?

Yes. Select the JPG and click Resize Image. There is no signup or watermark, and the file stays on your device while it is processed.

Photo size 50 KB: a common limit for application forms

Many online applications cap each photo and signature upload at 50 KB. That is two and a half times the room of a 20 KB limit, so a portrait with a clear face usually keeps its detail.

Reduce image size to 50 KB on your phone
