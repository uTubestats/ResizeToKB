Before you write: quick answers

How do I resize an image to 50 KB on my phone?

Open the 50 KB resizer, choose your photo, and tap Resize Image. The result downloads to your phone.

Why is my image still larger than the KB limit?

Very detailed photos and PNG files are hard to shrink. Choose JPG, crop the photo, and read our guide on how to compress an image to a specific KB size.

How do I increase photo size in KB when a form says the file is too small?

Use the increase image size in KB tool and type the minimum from the form.

Where can I find the SSC, JEE, UPSC, or RRB photo and signature sizes?

Read our guides for SSC CGL, JEE Main, UPSC, and RRB, then confirm the numbers in the official notice.

Is it safe to resize a passport or Aadhaar photo here?

Yes. The resizing runs inside your browser, so the photo is not uploaded. See our privacy policy.
