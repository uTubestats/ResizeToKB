How to Resize a Signature for Exam Forms (Scan, Crop, Compress)

Updated October 2026

Most exam forms ask for a scanned signature in JPG format with a small file size. A clean signature compresses well, so a few simple steps usually fix the upload.

Step 1: Sign on white paper

Use plain white paper and a black or dark blue pen. Avoid lined paper, since the lines add noise and make the file larger.

Step 2: Take a clear photo or scan

Use bright, even light with no shadow across the page. Hold the phone straight above the paper so the signature is not tilted or stretched.

Step 3: Crop close to the signature

Crop away empty paper so the signature fills the frame. Many forms expect a wide, short image, for example 236 × 79 pixels for SSC CGL as listed in 2026 guides.

Step 4: Resize to the KB limit

Use the resizer that matches your form:

  • SSC CGL signature (236 × 79 px, under 20KB)
  • JEE Main signature (under 100KB)
  • Any other limit: 20KB, 50KB, or 100KB

Common problems

  • Too light or faded: use a darker pen and retake the photo in better light.
  • Still too large: crop tighter, then resize again.
  • Wrong format: save as JPG unless the form says otherwise.

Always check your exam's official notification, because size limits differ between exams and change over time.

What a good signature scan looks like

A good scan has a pure white background, a dark continuous line, and no gray patches or shadows. The signature should fill most of the image, with only a small margin around it. If the edges look fuzzy when you zoom in, retake the photo closer and in better light before you resize.

Frequently asked questions

Can I type my signature instead?

Most exams ask for a handwritten signature, and some say typed or digital signatures are not allowed. Read your form's rules first.

What size should a signature be in KB?

It depends on the exam. Examples from 2026 guides include 10–20KB for SSC CGL and 10–100KB for JEE Main. Always check the current notification.

Why does my signature look pixelated after resizing?

The original was probably too small or too dark. Retake it at a higher quality, crop tightly, and avoid shrinking the file more than needed.

Should I use a pen or a marker?

Use a black or dark blue pen with a medium tip. Markers can bleed and make the signature too thick.

Quick recap

Sign on white paper, photograph or scan it in good light, crop close, resize to the size and KB limit your exam lists, save as JPG, and check the result before uploading.
