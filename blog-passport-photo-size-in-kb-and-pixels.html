Passport Photo Size in KB and Pixels: Common Sizes and How to Resize

Updated October 2026

Passport and visa photos follow strict rules, and rules differ by country. This guide shows common sizes and how to resize your photo for an online form.

Common passport photo sizes

Region (example)SizePixels
India, UK, EU and many others35 × 45 mm413 × 531 px at 300 DPI
United States2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm)600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 px for online upload

Many portals also set a maximum file size in KB. These limits change, so always read the instructions of the office or portal you are applying to.

How millimetres turn into pixels

Pixels = size in inches × DPI. A 35 mm side is about 1.38 inches, and at 300 DPI that is roughly 413 pixels. A 45 mm side is about 1.77 inches, or 531 pixels. This is why 413 × 531 is the standard digital size for a 35 × 45 mm photo.

Resize your photo

  1. Open the passport photo resizer, which is set to 413 × 531.
  2. Select a front-facing photo with a plain light background.
  3. Click Resize Image. The tool crops to fit instead of stretching your face.
  4. If the portal also has a KB limit, run the result through the 100KB tool or 50KB tool.

Photo tips

  • Face the camera, with a neutral expression and both eyes open.
  • Use even light with no shadows on the face or background.
  • Avoid filters, heavy retouching, and glare on glasses.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a passport photo with my phone?

Often yes, if the rules allow it. Use daylight, a plain wall, and hold the phone at eye level.

Why was my digital photo rejected?

Common reasons are the wrong dimensions, a cluttered background, shadows, or a file that is too large or too small.

Background and clothing

Most offices want a plain white or light background with no patterns. Wear everyday clothes in a color that contrasts with the background, and avoid white shirts on a white wall. Remove hats and head coverings unless they are worn for religious or medical reasons and your country allows them.

Checking your photo before you upload

  1. Zoom to 100% and look at your eyes. They should be sharp, with no red-eye or glare.
  2. Check that your head is centered and that there is space above it.
  3. Look for shadows behind your head or across your face.
  4. Confirm the pixel size and the KB size match the portal's rules.

Printing instead of uploading

If you need printed photos, keep the original large file and print at 300 DPI. A 413 × 531 pixel file prints at 35 × 45 mm. Do not enlarge a small image, because it will look soft. For printing, send the highest-quality copy to the shop and keep the small upload copy only for online forms.

Visa and ID photos

Visa, driving licence, and student ID photos each have their own rules. The method is the same: find the official size, set the pixels, then check the KB limit. If you are unsure, use the closest preset here and read the instructions on the portal before submitting.
