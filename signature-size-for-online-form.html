Signature Size for Online Form: Scan, Crop and Resize

Published 25 October 2026 · by ResizeToKB

The right signature size for online form uploads depends on the form, and it is always stated in the official instructions. This guide shows how to prepare a clean signature image that meets the limit.

Step 1: Sign on Plain White Paper

  • Use a dark blue or black pen.
  • Sign inside a clear rectangle with space around it.
  • Do not use lined paper.

Step 2: Take a Clean Photo or Scan

  • Use bright, even light and avoid shadows.
  • Hold the phone straight above the paper.
  • Keep the whole signature inside the frame.

Step 3: Crop and Resize

  1. Crop close to the signature in your gallery.
  2. Open the signature resizer.
  3. Enter the pixel size or KB limit from the form.
  4. Download the file and check it looks clear.

Signature Size for Online Form: Common Rejection Reasons

  • The file is larger than the limit.
  • The pixel size is different from the rule.
  • The ink is too pale or the picture is dark.
  • The signature is cut off at the edge.

Limits change from exam to exam and year to year, so always use the numbers from the current official notice.
