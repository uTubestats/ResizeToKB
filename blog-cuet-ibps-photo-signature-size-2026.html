CUET and IBPS Photo and Signature Size 2026: KB Limits Explained

Updated October 2026

Both CUET and IBPS check photo and signature files before they accept your application. Here are the limits reported in 2026 guides.

Sizes listed in 2026 guides

ExamPhotoSignature
CUET UG10–200 KB, JPG10–50 KB, JPG
IBPS20–50 KB, JPG, about 200 × 230 pxSee official notice

These numbers come from published guides and can change. Confirm every detail in the official notification before you upload.

Resize your photo

  1. For IBPS, open the IBPS photo resizer. It sets 200 × 230 pixels and keeps the file under 50KB.
  2. For CUET, use the 200KB tool for the photo.
  3. Download the JPG and check the size shown.

Resize your signature

Sign on white paper, take a bright photo, crop tightly, then use the 50KB tool or 20KB tool depending on the limit. See our signature guide.

Avoid rejection

  • Check format, size, and dimensions before you upload.
  • Use a recent photo with a clear face and plain background.
  • Keep the signature dark, sharp, and on a white background.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the same photo for both exams?

Often yes, if it meets each notice. Resize a separate copy for each form.

What if the portal says the file is invalid?

Check the format first (JPG), then the KB size, then the dimensions.

Step-by-step plan for your application

  1. Open the official notification and write down the size, format, and KB range for each file.
  2. Take a clear photo with a plain background, and sign on white paper.
  3. Resize each file with the matching tool and note the KB size shown.
  4. Rename the files if the form asks for specific names.
  5. Upload, check the preview, and save a backup copy.

Why file size ranges have a minimum

Portals set a minimum so that images are not blank or too small to identify you. If your result is below the minimum, use a sharper original with more detail and resize again. Do not simply enlarge a small image, because it will look blurry.

Keep your files organized

Many candidates apply to several exams. Save a folder with your photo, signature, and any certificates, and put the exam name and size in each file name. That way you can reuse the right files the next time and avoid uploading the wrong version.

Stay up to date

Exam bodies update size rules from year to year. Always compare the numbers in this guide with the latest official notice before you submit your form.
