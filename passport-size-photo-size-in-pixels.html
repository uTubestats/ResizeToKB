Passport Size Photo Size in Pixels and Centimeters

Published 25 October 2026 · by ResizeToKB

What is the passport size photo size in pixels? It depends on the country and the form. This guide explains how to convert centimeters and inches to pixels, so you can resize your photo correctly.

Passport Size Photo Size in Pixels at 300 DPI

Photo sizePixels at 300 DPI
35×45mm (3.5×4.5cm)about 413×531
2×2 inches600×600

Official offices set their own rules for head size, background and file type, so check them before you apply.

How to Convert Size to Pixels

  1. Change millimeters to inches by dividing by 25.4.
  2. Multiply the inches by the DPI, often 300.
  3. Round to the nearest whole number.

For example, 35mm is about 1.38 inches, and 1.38 × 300 is about 413 pixels.

How to Resize Your Passport Photo

  1. Crop your photo to the right shape with the face centered.
  2. Open the passport photo resizer.
  3. Enter the width and height from the form.
  4. If there is also a KB limit, use Target KB mode afterwards.

This tool changes only the size of the picture. It does not check head position or background rules.
