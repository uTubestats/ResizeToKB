How to Reduce Photo Size in KB on Mobile

Published 25 October 2026 · by ResizeToKB

Do you need to reduce photo size in KB on mobile for an online form? You do not need an app. A free browser tool can do it in less than a minute, and your photo stays on your phone.

Why Phone Photos Are So Large

Modern phone cameras take pictures with many millions of pixels, so a single photo can be several megabytes. Most forms only need a small file, so you must shrink it first.

Steps to Reduce Photo Size in KB on Mobile

  1. Open the image resizer in Chrome or Safari.
  2. Tap Select Image and choose a photo.
  3. Open Target KB and type the size you need, such as 50 or 100.
  4. Tap Resize Image and check the preview.
  5. Tap Download Image, then find the file in your Downloads folder or Files app.

Tips for Better Results

  • Take the photo in bright, even light. Clean photos compress better.
  • Crop away empty space before you resize.
  • Use JPG for photos, unless the form asks for another type.
  • Do not rename a PNG to JPG. Convert it with the tool instead.

Common Questions

Do I need to install an app?

No. The tool runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install.

Does it use a lot of mobile data?

Only the page itself loads. Your photo is not uploaded, so it uses no data for the image.

Where is my resized photo saved?

In your phone Downloads folder, or in the Files app on iPhone.
