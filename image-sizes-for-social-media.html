Image Sizes for Social Media

Published 25 October 2026 · by ResizeToKB

Using the right image sizes for social media helps your pictures stay sharp and avoids awkward cropping. This guide lists common sizes and how to use them.

Common Image Sizes for Social Media

UseSize (pixels)
Instagram post1080×1080
Instagram story1080×1920
YouTube thumbnail1280×720
Facebook link image1200×630
Facebook page cover820×312
WhatsApp profile picture400×400

Platforms can change their layouts, so treat these as reliable starting points and check the preview after you upload.

Tips for Using Social Media Image Sizes

  • Keep faces and text away from the edges, because apps crop pictures in different ways.
  • Use a square picture for profile photos, since many apps crop them into a circle.
  • Match the shape of the original photo to the target size to avoid stretching.
