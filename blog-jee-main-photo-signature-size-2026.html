JEE Main Photo and Signature Size 2026: KB Limits and How to Resize

Updated October 2026

The JEE Main application form checks your photo and signature file size before it accepts them. Here are the limits listed in 2026 guides and a simple way to meet them.

JEE Main photo and signature size

PhotoSignature
File size10–200 KB10–100 KB
Print size3.5 cm × 4.5 cm3.5 cm × 1.5 cm
FormatJPG / JPEGJPG / JPEG

Guides for the 2026 session noted two changes: the maximum photo size was lowered from 300KB to 200KB, and the maximum signature size was raised from 50KB to 100KB. Rules can change again, so confirm the numbers in the current official bulletin before you upload.

Resize your photo

  1. Open the JEE Main photo resizer.
  2. Select a recent passport-style photo with a plain background and a clear face.
  3. Click Resize Image and download the JPG. It stays under 200KB.

Resize your signature

  1. Sign on white paper with a dark pen.
  2. Photograph or scan it in good light and crop the empty space.
  3. Open the JEE Main signature resizer, select the file, and click Resize Image.

Tips

  • Keep the signature background white and the ink dark.
  • Do not use heavily edited or filtered photos.
  • If the form rejects the file, check the format first, then the size.

What the form checks

The upload step checks the file format, the size in KB, and sometimes the look of the image. A correct size alone does not guarantee acceptance, so make sure the face is clear and the signature is readable. Do not upload a group photo, a selfie with filters, or a photo with a hat or dark glasses unless the instructions allow it.

Prepare the photo in four steps

  1. Choose a recent, front-facing passport-style photo with a plain background.
  2. Open the JEE Main photo page, select the picture, and tap Resize Image.
  3. Look at the size under the preview. The file should be under 200 KB and not tiny, because the form lists a minimum as well.
  4. Download the JPG and keep the original photo as a backup.

Making the signature clear

Sign on white paper with a dark pen, in your normal handwriting. Photograph it in even light, crop the empty paper, and then resize. If the signature looks too pale after resizing, retake the photo in brighter light instead of lowering the quality again.

Common mistakes with JEE Main uploads

  • Using a photo with filters, strong shadows, or a coloured background.
  • Uploading a PNG or a screenshot instead of a JPG.
  • Squeezing the file far below the minimum size, which loses detail.
  • Using last year's size limits without checking the new bulletin.

If the size is wrong

When the form says the file is too large, lower the limit in the KB tool by a few KB and try again. When it says the file is too small, use the increase image size in KB tool, or retake the picture at a higher quality.

Frequently asked questions

What is the JEE Main photo size in KB?

Guides for the 2026 session list 10–200 KB for the photo in JPG format. Confirm this in the current bulletin.

What is the JEE Main signature size?

2026 guides list 10–100 KB in JPG format on a white background.

My photo is too small, what do I do?

If the file is under the minimum, retake or scan it at a higher quality so there is more detail, then resize it again.

Can I use the same photo as my passport?

Many candidates do, as long as it is recent and meets the notification rules. Use a clean copy rather than a photo of a printed picture.

Final check

Open the downloaded file on your phone and zoom in. Your face should be sharp and the signature should be dark and continuous. Then upload and confirm the preview before you submit the form. Keep a copy of the final files in case you need to correct the form later.
