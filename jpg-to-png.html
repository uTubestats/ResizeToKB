How to convert JPG to PNG

  1. Select your JPG file.
  2. Check that Convert to is set to PNG.
  3. Click Convert Image, then download the PNG.

Why people convert JPG to PNG

PNG is lossless, so you can edit and save it again without adding new compression damage. It is also the usual choice for logos, icons, and screenshots, and for software that asks for PNG files.

What the conversion cannot do

A JPG already lost some detail when it was first saved, and PNG cannot bring it back. The PNG will look the same as the JPG, but the file will usually be larger. A JPG also has no transparent areas, so the PNG will not have any either.

When JPG to PNG makes sense

Choose PNG when you will edit the picture again, when software asks for PNG, or when you need a lossless copy of the current look. Keep the JPG when you only need a small file for email, a form, or a website.

FAQ: Convert JPG to PNG Online Free Without Losing Quality, No Upload for Forms, Exams, and Job Portals

Will converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. The picture looks the same, because the detail lost in the JPG cannot be restored. You gain a lossless format for future edits.

Why is my PNG bigger than the JPG?

PNG stores every pixel without lossy compression, which makes photo files much larger. If size matters, keep the JPG.

Does a PNG made from a JPG have a transparent background?

No. A JPG has no transparency, so the converted PNG keeps the solid background.

How do I convert JPEG to PNG online free on mobile without losing quality?

Select the JPEG, keep PNG chosen in the Convert to menu, and tap Convert Image. The PNG looks the same as the JPEG, because PNG saves every pixel without new compression.
