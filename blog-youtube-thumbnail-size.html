YouTube Thumbnail Size: 1280x720 Dimensions, File Size and Tips

Updated October 2026

A clear thumbnail with the right size helps your video get clicks. The standard size is simple to remember, and easy to meet with a free resizer.

YouTube thumbnail specs

SettingValue
Resolution1280 × 720 px
Aspect ratio16:9
Minimum width640 px
File sizeUnder 2 MB
FormatsJPG, PNG, GIF

These are the sizes YouTube has listed for years. Check YouTube Help if you see an upload error.

How to resize your thumbnail

  1. Open the YouTube thumbnail resizer.
  2. Select your image and click Resize Image.
  3. If the file is over 2MB, save it as JPG or use the 200KB tool.

Design tips

  • Use large, readable text of three to five words.
  • Keep important elements away from the bottom right corner, where the video length badge appears.
  • Use bright, high-contrast colors and a clear face or subject.
  • Check how it looks small, since many viewers see it on a phone.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use JPG or PNG?

JPG gives smaller files for photos. PNG is better for sharp text and graphics, but can pass the 2MB limit.

Why does my thumbnail look blurry?

It was probably smaller than 1280 × 720 and stretched. Start from a larger original and resize down.

Thumbnail checklist

  1. Create the image at 1280 × 720 or larger in 16:9.
  2. Keep the file under 2 MB in JPG or PNG.
  3. Use a clear subject and text that is readable at small size.
  4. Avoid placing key text in the bottom right corner.
  5. Preview it on your phone before uploading.

Common thumbnail mistakes

  • Too much small text that cannot be read in a feed.
  • Low contrast between text and background.
  • Stretching a small image, which causes blur.
  • Misleading images that do not match the video, which can hurt viewer trust.

Other YouTube image sizes

Channel banners, profile pictures, and Shorts covers each have their own sizes. The pattern is the same: find the current size in YouTube Help, resize your image to match, and keep important details near the middle so they are not cropped on small screens.
