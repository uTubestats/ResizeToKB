About ResizeToKB

ResizeToKB is a free online tool for changing the size of pictures. Many people need to meet a limit such as 50KB or 100KB for a form, or fit a photo to Instagram or YouTube, and we built simple pages for those jobs.

What we focus on

  • Privacy: the resizing happens in your browser, so your images are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Clarity: each page explains one task, with the size already filled in where it is well known.
  • Honesty: for exams and official forms we tell you to use the limits in the official notice, because they change.

ResizeToKB is an independent project. Questions or ideas? Contact us.
