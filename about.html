About ResizeToKB

ResizeToKB is a free online tool that changes the size of pictures. It helps you meet a file size limit such as 50KB or 100KB, or fit a photo to the shape a social media app expects.

Why We Built ResizeToKB

Many forms, job portals and apps reject a photo because the file is too large or the wrong size. The error message rarely explains how to fix it. We built simple pages that do one job each, so you can fix the problem in a minute.

What You Can Do Here

  • Resize by pixels, by percentage, or to a target size in KB.
  • Use ready sizes for Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and WhatsApp, and for passport-size photos.
  • Save the result as JPG, WebP or PNG, and preview it before you download it.

How the Tool Works

The tool runs inside your web browser. Your image is drawn onto a canvas on your own device and saved again at the size you choose. In Target KB mode, the tool lowers the quality in small steps. If the file is still too big, it also reduces the pixel size until it fits under your limit. Because of this, very small targets can make a photo look softer.

Who It Is For

  • Students and job seekers who fill in online application forms.
  • Creators and small businesses who post on social media.
  • Anyone who needs to send a photo by email or messaging apps.

Our Principles

  • Privacy: your images are not uploaded to our servers. Read the Privacy Policy.
  • Honesty: exam and government forms set their own limits, and these change. We ask you to use the numbers from the official notice, and we do not guess them for you.
  • Clear guides: our blog explains image sizes and formats in plain language.

ResizeToKB is an independent project. It is not connected to any exam body, government office or social network. If you have a question or an idea for a new tool, please contact us.
