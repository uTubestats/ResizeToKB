About ResizeToKB

A free online image resizer for KB, pixels, and percentage, made for forms, exams, and everyday uploads.

What ResizeToKB does

ResizeToKB helps you change the size of a picture so it fits what a form, website, or app asks for. You can resize an image to a KB limit such as 20 KB, 50 KB, or 100 KB, set an exact width and height in pixels, scale a picture by percentage, or raise a file to a minimum size. There are ready-made pages for social media sizes, passport photos, and exam photos and signatures, and extra tools to convert, rotate, flip, and resize many images at once.

Why we built it

A rejected upload is frustrating, especially on the last day of an application. Most people do not need photo editing software. They need one number, the right size, applied quickly on their phone. We built the site around that need: open a page, choose a photo, and download a file that meets the limit.

How your photos stay private

The tools run inside your browser. When you resize a photo, the work happens on your device, and the picture is not sent to our servers. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not add watermarks. Our privacy policy explains the details.

How we keep the sizes accurate

Exam and form requirements change every year. For our exam pages, we compare the sizes listed in recent guides, such as the photo and signature rules for SSC, JEE Main, UPSC, RRB, IBPS, CUET, and PAN, and we tell you clearly when sources disagree. We always link back to the official notice as the final authority. If you find a wrong size, please tell us and we will correct it.

Who uses ResizeToKB

  • Students and job seekers who need to resize a photo or signature for an online application.
  • People who need a passport or ID photo at the right pixel size and file size.
  • Creators who need the right size for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or WhatsApp.
  • Anyone who wants to compress a photo for email without losing too much quality.

What we do not do

We do not store your pictures, sell your files, or ask you to sign up. We are an independent site and are not part of any exam body, government office, or social media company. Names of exams and platforms are used only to describe what each page is for.
