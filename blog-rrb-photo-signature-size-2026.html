RRB Photo and Signature Size 2026 in KB and Pixels

Updated October 2026

Railway recruitment forms (NTPC, ALP, Technician, Group D, and others) have strict image rules, and the rules are different in different notices. This guide explains what recent 2026 guides list and how to prepare your files, but the notice of your own recruitment is the final word.

What recent RRB notices ask for

PhotographSignature
How it is providedLive capture with a webcam or phone camera, in recent noticesScanned image upload
FormatJPG or JPEGJPG or JPEG
File sizeNot applicable to live capture30 to 49 KB
DimensionsSet by the live captureAt least 140 × 60 pixels
Other rulesPlain background, face visibleBlack ink on white paper, at least 100 DPI

Older recruitment cycles listed a photo upload of about 20 to 50 KB, at 35 × 45 mm and 100 DPI. Do not use an old size for a new notice. Check whether your application uses live capture or an upload.

How to prepare the signature

  1. Sign in running handwriting with a black pen on clean white paper.
  2. Photograph or scan it in even light, then crop close to the signature.
  3. Open the reduce image size in KB tool, type 49, and click Resize Image.
  4. Check that the file is at least 30 KB. If it is smaller, use the increase image size in KB tool with a minimum of 30.
  5. Confirm the image is at least 140 × 60 pixels with the pixels tool.

If your recruitment asks for a photo upload

When the notice asks for an uploaded photograph, follow its pixel size and KB range. A 35 × 45 mm photo with a plain background is typical. Use the 50 KB resizer or the exact range from your notice.

Common reasons for rejection

  • A typed or digital signature instead of a handwritten one.
  • A signature file outside the KB range or below the minimum pixels.
  • A wrong format, such as a PNG.
  • Using the photo rules of an older notice.

Checklist before the final submit

  • Read the notice for your exact recruitment, such as NTPC, ALP, Technician, or Group D.
  • Prepare the signature as a JPG within the KB range and above the minimum pixels.
  • Check whether the photo is live captured, and use a device with a working camera if so.
  • Save the final signature and keep a backup copy.
  • Do the upload early, because portals slow down near the last date.

Different notices, different rules

Railway recruitment is run by regional boards and by central notices, and the image rules can change from one notice to the next. A size that was correct last year may be wrong now. If a guide, a video, and the official notice disagree, follow the notice.

Fixing a signature that is too small or too large

If the file is above 49 KB, lower the limit in the KB tool by a few KB and try again. If it is below 30 KB, use the increase tool, or scan at a higher resolution so there is more detail. If it is too small in pixels, scan it again rather than enlarging it, because enlarging only makes it blurry.

Frequently asked questions

What is the RRB signature size in KB?

Recent guides list 30 to 49 KB as JPG, but check your own recruitment notice because it can change.

Is the RRB photo uploaded or captured live?

Recent notices use live capture for the photo and an upload for the signature. Older ones used an upload, so read your notice.

What is the minimum signature size in pixels?

Guides list at least 140 pixels wide and 60 pixels high. Confirm it in the notice.
