Why WebP images do not open everywhere

WebP is common on websites because the files are small, but some forms, older apps, and email tools only accept JPG or PNG. Converting to JPG makes the picture work in almost every place.

Quality and size

Both formats are lossy for photos, so a conversion at 92 percent quality looks nearly the same. The JPG may be slightly larger than the WebP. If you need a KB limit, follow up with the reduce image size in KB tool.

Where WebP images come from

Many websites and apps save pictures as WebP to load faster. When you download such a picture, you may end up with a WebP file that your phone gallery or a form cannot use. This tool turns it into a standard JPG in one step.
